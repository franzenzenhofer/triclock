Privacy

Triclock collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no cookies, and nothing follows you across apps or websites. This page is for the clock at triclock.franzai.com and the Triclock app for iPhone and iPad.

The app

The Triclock app for iPhone and iPad runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests. It saves nothing about you. While it is open it keeps the screen on, so it can be left on display like a desk clock; when you leave the app your device sleeps as usual.

Sharing

When you share a time, Triclock hands a picture and a link to your device's own share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share with; Triclock sends nothing anywhere itself.

The website

The website is delivered by Cloudflare. As with every website, your device's IP address is used to deliver the page; Triclock does not store it or anything else about your visit. Cloudflare's own policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

Children

Triclock asks for no personal information and collects none, from anyone.
